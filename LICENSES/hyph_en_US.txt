tools/data/hyph_en_US.dic and web/assets/hyph_en_US.dic are the US English
Liang hyphenation patterns as packaged by the hunspell/hyphen project
(https://github.com/hunspell/hyphen), converted from Franklin Mark Liang's
original TeX hyphenation patterns (hyphen.tex / ushyphmax.tex).

original license header from the source package is reproduced here because
the compiled .dic ships without it.
