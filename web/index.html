Getting it onto a Game Boy
You need a flashcart — a cartridge with a microSD slot. Copy the .gba onto the card (FAT32, any folder) and pick it from the cart menu. Your place saves automatically. In the reader: A/→ forward, ← back, ↑/↓ chapters, SELECT palette, B back to the library.
EZ-Flash Air~$40, the budget pick (writes the ROM to the cart before playing)
EverDrive GBA Mini~$99, premium drag-and-drop
Buy from the makers' resellers to dodge clones. Any GBA plays it — the backlit SP (AGS-101) has the nicest screen; the frontlit AGS-001 pairs well with the high-contrast palette.
No Game Boy?
It runs perfectly in an emulator: mGBA on desktop, Delta on iOS, or Lemuroid on Android. Drop the .gba in and read.
How it works
Everything happens in your browser — your books never leave your machine. The page reads the EPUB, and a C typesetting engine compiled to WebAssembly does the real work: justified type with kerning and hyphenation, laid out once so the Game Boy just replays it. The same engine builds the ROM and runs on the console.
