Getting it onto a Game Boy
You need a flashcart — a cartridge with a microSD slot. Copy the .gba onto the card (FAT32, any folder) and pick it from the cart menu. Your place saves automatically. EZ-Flash Air or EverDrive GBA Mini are good options
Controls
On the Game Boy Advance: A/→ page forward, ← back, ↑/↓ jump chapters, SELECT toggles the two palettes (paperback and night), B returns to your library. In the library, ↑/↓ browse the stack and A opens a book.
No Game Boy?
It runs perfectly in an emulator: mGBA on desktop, Delta on iOS, or Lemuroid on Android. Drop the .gba in and read.
